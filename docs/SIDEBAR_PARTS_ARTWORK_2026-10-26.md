# Sidebar artwork and parts scenes — 2026-10-26

## User-requested scope
Five dedicated full-bleed parts scenes replace the flat icon-plus-strip headings:
engine, brakes, suspension/chassis, electrical and lighting. The other three
illustrated parts headings remain unchanged. The title is real HTML, the scene
is decorative and contain-fitted, and category data/search links remain unchanged.

The left sidebar now uses one 22px artwork slot for transport, parts, services,
help and authenticated account links. Parts were reduced from 28px. Original
approved transport/service artwork is reused at the navigation size. Seven new
identities cover dashboard, wallet, settings, notifications, orders, store and support.
The former 33px empty-icon indent on services/help is removed, so every section
shares the same left alignment. New small assets are only 96px WebP. User names, roles, counters and permissions
remain real data, not raster artwork.

Five authored vector flags precede auction destinations. The Europe destination
retains its legacy DE filter key and displays a European flag. The short 700ms
single wave is interaction-triggered and disabled for reduced-motion preference.
No automatic looping animation or external flag service is used.

## Composition analysis
Every scene places a recognizable assembly at the right and deep petrol negative
space at the left for accessible text. The engine scene shows a genuine engine
silhouette instead of the previous generic parts collage. Brake rotor/caliper,
strut/control arm, battery/alternator and headlamp give the other sections
independent visual identities. The sidebar icons share enamel, titanium and
copper but keep distinctive silhouettes. Decorative backgrounds do not imply
availability, condition or fitment of sellers' real products.
